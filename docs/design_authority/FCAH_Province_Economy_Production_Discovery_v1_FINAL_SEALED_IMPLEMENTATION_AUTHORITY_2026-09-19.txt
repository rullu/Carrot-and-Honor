4. CORE PROVINCE MODEL
======================================================================

Every Province has three conceptual layers.

A. LAND / COUNTRYSIDE
Contains:
- fixed geography / terrain facts;
- background/untracked mundane access;
- generated tracked opportunities;
- farms, fields, mines, quarries, orchards, pastures, forestry and similar exploitation
  sites;
- other rural systems only when separately designed.

B. STRATEGIC CITY
Contains deliberate ruler-level investment in:
- processing;
- workshops;
- commerce;
- administration;
- military organization;
- religion;
- scholarship;
- advanced / prestige production.

C. REALM NETWORK
Later connects Provinces through:
- Realm-level access;
- trade/import/export;
- strategic supply relationships;
- overseas expeditions;
- Realm goods-set / achievement systems.

CORE RULE
Land exposes / produces.
City refines / crafts / organizes / weaponizes / commercializes.
Realm network converts local specialization into wider strategic access.

NO SECOND COUNTRYSIDE SLOT GRID
- There is no generic `6 rural slots`, rural points or manually filled countryside board.
- The opportunity/deposit/land condition is the limiting slot: `the deposit is the slot`.
- Countryside sites do not consume strategic city slots.

STRATEGIC CITY CAPACITY
- CURRENT PROTOTYPE STANDARD: 10 strategic slots per Province.
- 8 and 12 remain deliberate comparison/stress-test values.
- Final permanent count is NOT locked yet.
- 10 must be data/configuration, not a permanent architecture constant where avoidable.

VISUAL ABSTRACTION
- Province UI is authoritative for exact state.
- World-map economic visuals are selective hints.
- Mechanics never depend on fitting every tracked object physically into a small Province.
- Visual defensive architecture is not proof of mechanical Fortification Works.

5. ECONOMIC TAXONOMY
======================================================================

Every resource-like noun belongs to a mechanical class.
Do not throw all nouns into one generic resource list.

A. FIXED GEOGRAPHY / PHYSICAL CONDITION
- intrinsic/handcrafted world fact;
- does not consume the generated 0-3 opportunity count.

Current examples:
- Coast;
- Major / Large River;
- Mountains;
- Hills;
- Wetlands / Marsh;
- Drylands / arid terrain;
- fixed biome/climate identity;
- Natural Harbour / exceptional harbour potential where authored;
- exceptional river-valley geography where physically represented.

Ancient Forest is not a second mechanical opportunity. It may be flavour/visual/lore
identity for Deep Forest.

B. BACKGROUND / UNTRACKED BASELINE ACCESS
Mundane availability sufficient for ordinary civilization.
Normally omitted from Province resource UI.

Current background access:
- ordinary Timber;
- Firewood;
- ordinary construction Stone / rubble;
- ordinary basic metalwork / ironware for normal tools, basic buildings and ordinary
  soldiers;
- ordinary dirt / crude household clay for trivial uses;
- common sand;
- ordinary tiny-scale herbs, bees and foraging where appropriate.

C. TRACKED GENERATED PROVINCE OPPORTUNITY
Replay-varying strategic land/deposit/suitability.
Consumes one of the Province's 0-3 generated opportunity positions.
Does not produce anything until an appropriate exploitation site exists.

D. GEOGRAPHY-DERIVED SITE / OUTPUT
Enabled by fixed geography rather than the 0-3 generator.
Example: Fishery from suitable aquatic geography.

E. DERIVED / PROCESSED GOOD OR CAPABILITY
Produced by countryside sites, city buildings, trade, or other systems.
Does not consume an opportunity position merely because it exists.

F. REALM / CULTURAL / TRADE ACCESS
Availability determined by Realm/culture/trade rather than a Province deposit roll.
Example: Camels.

G. OFF-MAP / OVERSEAS EXPEDITION GOOD
Cannot be generated by playable-map Province geography.
Examples already accepted:
- Ebony / exotic overseas woods;
- Exotic Spices.

H. ERA-DEFERRED / CUT-FOR-NOW
Excluded until another system gives it a clear job.
Current examples:
- Lead;
- Peat;
- Sulfur;
- Saltpeter;
- Coal.

7. IMPORTANT RESOURCE-FAMILY BINDINGS
======================================================================

FERTILE LAND
One broad opportunity may support simultaneously:
- Grain Fields;
- Carrot Fields;
- Root-Crop Fields;
- Legume Fields;
- Vegetable Fields / Gardens;
- Oilseed / Seed cultivation.

No hidden rural-capacity number is introduced.
Fruit, Grapes, Hops, Flax, Hemp, Herbs and Dye Plants remain separate opportunities.

CARROTS — DUAL SYSTEM
- Treasury Carrots = Realm currency / unit of account.
- Physical carrots = edible staple crop.
- They are distinct state/content concepts.
- Physical Carrot Fields come from Fertile Land.
- Gold remains a physical material/ore, not the treasury currency.

FOREST
- Ordinary Timber / Firewood = background.
- Deep Forest = exceptional extensive forestry opportunity.
- Rare Timber = separate exceptional wood opportunity.
- Deep Forest and Rare Timber may coexist but do not require one another.
- Resin is derived from serious Deep-Forest forestry; no mandatory Resin Collector site.
- Charcoal is derived from ordinary wood processing and does NOT require Deep Forest.
- Ebony is off-map expedition cargo, never domestic Rare Timber.

PASTORAL / HIDES
Pastureland -> Sheep Pasture -> Wool + Hide Supply.
- Sheep/Wool/Hides are not separate opportunity rolls.
- Hide Supply is an abstract herd-lifecycle by-product.
- No Meat, Mutton, Butcher, Slaughterhouse or Hide Deposit system is introduced.
- Hide Supply may feed local Tanner/Parchment production later.
- finished Leather/Parchment may become Realm-accessible according to downstream scope.

HORSES
Horse-Breeding Conditions -> Horse Stud -> domestic breeding capability / horse stock.
- Horses are not a generated deposit.
- breeding capability/tradition remains local;
- actual horse access may later move through Realm/trade systems.

CAMELS
- no generated camel opportunity;
- access may come from culture/region/trade/diplomacy;
- Stables organize/maintain mounted capability; Stables do not create animals.

BEES
Bee-rich Meadows -> Apiary -> Honey + Beeswax.
Honey and Beeswax are outputs, not opportunity rolls.

STONE / CLAY / GLASS
- ordinary Stone = background everywhere for normal construction;
- Limestone / Marble / Slate = tracked special stone opportunities;
- serious Clay Deposit = tracked; crude household clay remains background;
- ordinary Bricks are accepted as a Realm-accessible finished good once genuinely produced;
- common sand = background; Glassmaking Sand = tracked specialist opportunity;
- source-local extraction/industry remains distinct from finished-good access.

FISH
- retained in FCAH despite older exclusion language;
- Fish does NOT occupy the 0-3 opportunity pool;
- suitable aquatic fixed geography -> Fishery / Fishing Village -> Fresh Fish;
- Fresh Fish is mainly local food/economic capability;
- preserved Salted/Smoked Fish may later be Realm-accessible/tradeable;
- Fish is optional to Realm viability;
- no basic building/unit should require Fish;
- `Rich Fishing Waters` is retired as a normal random opportunity for now.

HERBS / SPICES
- Herbs = domestic tracked agricultural opportunity.
- Exotic Spices = off-map expedition/external-trade good.

FLAX / HEMP
- remain separate tracked opportunities;
- Flax primarily supports linen/finer textiles;
- Hemp primarily supports rope/canvas/heavy-duty/maritime uses.

MUSHROOMS / NUTS / REEDS / PEAT / LEAD
- Mushrooms = contextual/background foraging, not strategic deposit;
- Nuts = contextual/orchard/woodland/agricultural development, not separate opportunity;
- Reeds = contextual Wetland/river output, not separate opportunity;
- Peat = deferred until fuel design gives it a distinct role;
- Lead = deferred until technology/content gives it a distinct role.

8. OPPORTUNITY GENERATION RULES
======================================================================

8.1 COUNT PER PROVINCE
Prototype distribution:
- 0 opportunities: 20%
- 1 opportunity: 45%
- 2 opportunities: 30%
- 3 opportunities: 5%
- >3 forbidden.

Count is rolled first. Content is selected second.

8.2 SAME COUNT TABLE EVERYWHERE
No count modifier from:
- capital status;
- Realm identity;
- Powerhouse status;
- owner;
- terrain;
- climate;
- region;
- wealth;
- existing city development.

8.3 GLOBAL RARITY, NOT REGIONAL RARITY
- opportunity types may have different global rarity weights;
- the same opportunity weight applies in every Province;
- no mountain Iron bonus;
- no southern Grapes bonus;
- no Realm/culture/capital ownership weighting.

Exact canonical weights are deferred configuration and MUST NOT be invented by Codex.

8.4 DISTINCT OPPORTUNITIES
No duplicate exact opportunity inside one Province.
Broad + specific combinations such as Deep Forest + Rare Timber are valid.
No generic combination blacklist exists unless a true contradiction is explicitly authored.

8.5 SOFT ANTI-CLUSTERING
Rare/high-value opportunities receive soft anti-clustering.
- no hard universal distance law;
- nearby rare pairs remain possible;
- exact rare tags / penalty strength / algorithmic tuning are deferred configuration.

Implementation may provide a deterministic configurable mechanism and fixture data.
It must not invent a final balance table.

8.6 NO EXACT WORLD QUOTAS
No rule such as exactly four Gold deposits.
Normal campaign totals vary.

8.7 NON-DEPLETION
Underlying generated opportunity does not permanently vanish through ordinary exploitation.
Sites may be disrupted; the opportunity remains.

======================================================================
9. WORLD-PRESENCE / VISIBLE SAFETY FLOORS
======================================================================

Safety floors are repair safeguards, not target distributions and not Realm guarantees.
Normal generation runs first; repair only catches pathological low-presence seeds.

For the current 100-Province prototype:

FERTILE LAND
- minimum true world presence: 5 Provinces;
- minimum visible Day 1: 4 Provinces.

IRON DEPOSIT
- minimum true world presence: 2 Provinces;
- minimum visible Day 1: 1 Province.

No other opportunity has a floor by default.

REPAIR RULES
- world-level and ownership-blind;
- never guarantee a resource to a specific Realm;
- preserve max 3 per Province;
- preserve no-duplicate rules;
- preserve anti-clustering where possible;
- prefer adding to a Province with fewer than 3 opportunities;
- if every suitable Province is full, deterministically replace a sensible common /
  non-essential opportunity rather than creating a fourth;
- exact non-100-Province scaling remains tuning.

======================================================================
10. HIDDEN OPPORTUNITIES
======================================================================

Most generated opportunities are visible/known to their owning Realm on Day 1.
`Visible Day 1` does NOT require universal foreign omniscience.

HIDDEN START RULE
- Province gets one hidden-opportunity roll if it has one or more generated opportunities;
- at most one hidden opportunity per Province;
- if the roll succeeds, choose one eligible tracked opportunity at equal odds;
- same hidden probability in every Province;
- no modifier from capital, Realm, terrain, region, remoteness or resource count;
- fixed geography is never hidden;
- exact hidden probability is DEFERRED tuning and MUST NOT be silently hard-coded as 10%.

ORDER
1. fixed geography exists;
2. roll opportunity count;
3. select distinct opportunities;
4. apply anti-clustering / world repair;
5. enforce true world safety floors;
6. perform hidden selection while respecting visible-Day-1 safety floors;
7. run dependent starting-site logic afterward.

VISIBLE FLOOR RULE
If hiding an essential opportunity would push its visible count below the locked visible
floor, that instance is not eligible to hide. Choose another eligible opportunity if one
exists; otherwise the Province remains with no hidden opportunity.

SITE-VISIBILITY RULE
No starting exploitation site may advertise a resource that is still hidden to the owning
Realm.
- RNG starting sites require visible/known source.
- authored resource-exploitation sites make their source owner-known and remove it from
  hidden eligibility.

DISCOVERY reveals existing truth. It never spawns a convenient new resource.

======================================================================
12. CAMPAIGN-START COUNTRYSIDE SITES
======================================================================

Normal start:
- opportunities exist;
- developed exploitation sites usually do not.

Two possible pre-existing sources:
A. explicit authored canonical/historical exploitation site;
B. deterministic 4% RNG chance per Province for one eligible base exploitation site.

4% RULE
- same chance for every Province;
- no capital / Realm / Powerhouse / wealth / terrain modifier;
- independent per Province;
- no Realm/world quota;
- maximum one RNG-created starting countryside site per Province;
- authored exploitation site(s) may exceed one but suppress the Province's 4% resource-site
  RNG entirely;
- unrelated authored non-resource countryside content does not suppress this roll;
- hidden opportunity cannot receive an RNG site;
- starting sites are base form, fully constructed, active, revealed and mechanically real;
- valuable resource does not make the base site `advanced`: Gold Mine is still a base mine.

DAY-1 ELIGIBLE POOL
Any straightforward BASIC site in Section 11 may participate if its source is valid and
visible/known, including Fishery from fixed aquatic geography.

FAMILY-FIRST SELECTION
Do NOT choose equally among all eligible site nouns.

Algorithm:
1. roll the Province's 4% site chance;
2. if fail -> no RNG site;
3. if success -> collect eligible source/opportunity families;
4. exclude hidden/invalid sources;
5. choose one eligible source family at equal probability;
6. if that family exposes several eligible basic site types, choose one equally inside
   the family;
7. create that one base site.

If no eligible source family exists after a successful 4% roll, create no site.
Do not transfer the roll to another Province and do not violate prerequisites.

This prevents Fertile Land from receiving six lottery tickets merely because it exposes
six site nouns.

13.7 BUILDING / INSTITUTION / TRADITION SEPARATION
Building = physical infrastructure/place.
Institution = organized social body.
Tradition = accumulated local practice/capability/history.

Therefore Merchant Guild and Craft Guild are primarily Institutions, not automatic slot
buildings. A physical Guildhall may later exist without collapsing this distinction.

13.8 BROADLY BUILDABLE VS SOURCE-SENSITIVE

- Smithy / Metalworks does NOT require tracked Iron merely for ordinary smithing;
- Woodworks does NOT require Deep Forest / Rare Timber merely for ordinary carpentry.

Source-sensitive specialist industries include, under current principle:

15. LOCAL SOURCE / REALM ACCESS / IMPORT PRINCIPLE
======================================================================

A. BACKGROUND CIVILIZATION ACCESS
Basic civilization does not fail because a Province lacks a resource icon.
Ordinary wood, ordinary stone and ordinary metalwork are available at baseline as defined
above.

B. TRACKED RESOURCE = SPECIAL DEVELOPMENT POTENTIAL
Tracked resource means serious exploitable local capacity and identity, not literal
existence of the substance nowhere else.

C. SOURCE-BOUND SPECIALIST INDUSTRY STAYS LOCAL
For source-bound resources:
- extraction is local;
- source-bound specialist industry/mastery/tradition remains local unless a future rule
  explicitly spreads it;
- importing raw material does not automatically reproduce source capability elsewhere.

D. IMPORTS ARE HAND-AUTHORED BY USE
Imports may satisfy specifically designated:
- consumption;
- construction;
- equipment;
- trade;
- Realm-level access;
- other explicit downstream uses.

There is NO universal rule that imported good = every local prerequisite satisfied.

E. PROCESSED/FINISHED OUTPUT SCOPE IS PER CONTENT
A downstream good may be:
- Province-local;
- Realm-accessible;
- trade/import satisfiable;
- restricted to local recruitment;
- available only for defined downstream uses.

Exact finished-good scope is the next major design pass and is NOT required to implement
this milestone's generation/state foundation.

Already accepted examples:
- ordinary Bricks can become Realm-accessible once genuinely produced;
- Leather/Parchment may become Realm-accessible finished goods;
- preserved fish may become Realm-accessible/tradeable;
- finished glass products may become Realm-accessible according to catalogue scope.

16. DISCOVERY / KNOWLEDGE CANON
======================================================================

These rules are SEALED design canon, but full runtime survey/passive-discovery behavior is
OUT OF SCOPE for the next narrow implementation slice unless separately authorized.

OBJECTIVE TRUTH VS REALM KNOWLEDGE
- opportunities exist objectively in Province state;
- hidden-resource knowledge may differ by Realm;
- one global `resource_known` boolean is insufficient for the eventual complete system.

ACTIVE SURVEY
- generic `Survey Province`, never Search-for-Gold etc.;
- only actual owner may order normal survey;
- temporary military occupation is insufficient;
- completion guarantees a final answer: reveal hidden opportunity or confirm none;
- survey cannot arbitrarily fail after completion;
- committed cost/duration are fixed and shown once ordered;
- duration/cost may depend on Province-facing difficulty but not the hidden result;
- gradual payment;
- insufficient funds auto-pause without progress loss;
- manual cancel = indefinite pause, not reroll;
- loss of ownership stops spending and preserves progress;
- reacquisition requires explicit Resume;
- no progress decay;
- multiple simultaneous Realm surveys allowed;
- completed negative result is permanent Realm-specific knowledge;
- no negative-survey transfer on conquest.

PASSIVE DISCOVERY
- independent alternative to active survey;
- contextual parchment/event/report channels;
- reveals existing positive truth only;
- small baseline chance exists for owned hidden opportunity;
- context may raise chance;
- periodic/event-based, not per-frame/per-day invisible spam;
- continues while an owned Province's active survey is paused for lack of funding;
- no guaranteed discovery deadline;
- exact cadence/chances remain later tuning.

If passive discovery reveals the hidden opportunity while an active or funding-paused
survey is still valid and the Realm still owns the Province:
- reveal immediately;
- resolve/close that survey;
- do not charge future unpaid survey cost.

The historical `ever discovered` trace affects passive rediscovery only. It does not
reduce active-survey cost or duration.

CONQUEST / KNOWLEDGE
- secret undeveloped knowledge does not automatically transfer on conquest;
- a real existing exploitation site reveals its represented resource to the actual new
  owner because the site is physical Province state;
- temporary occupation alone grants no special resource knowledge;
- both positive discoveries and negative survey conclusions remain remembered by the
  discovering Realm after later loss;
- no generic formal buy/sell/share-resource-map diplomatic action.

HISTORICAL REDISCOVERY TRACE
- once an opportunity was discovered by any Realm, keep a binary `ever discovered`
  historical trace;
- later unknown owners get a small passive rediscovery chance bonus;
- no decay;
- no stacking;
- passive only, no active-survey discount.

FOREIGN INFORMATION
- positive hidden opportunity may become known through plausible spies/merchants/
  travellers/diplomatic/narrative channels;
- negative survey conclusion normally remains private;
- knowledge changes neither ownership nor site state;
- the Province owner is not automatically notified merely because another Realm learned
  the positive information.

17. WARFARE / SITE / BUILDING PERSISTENCE
======================================================================

COUNTRYSIDE
- sites may be raided/pillaged/devastated;
- disruption may remove downstream availability temporarily;
- normal raid does not permanently delete site;
- site proceeds through recovery;
- developed site persists through ownership change, including damage state;
- actual new owner knows the resource represented by that physical site.

STRATEGIC CITY
- buildings normally persist through ordinary ownership change/warfare;
- normal warfare does not randomly erase them;
- normal generated strategic buildings are intended to be removable/replaceable later;
- explicitly authored landmarks may later be protected/non-removable;
- exact demolition/protection mechanics remain future design;
- exceptional destruction requires a separately designed rule.

TEMPORARY OCCUPATION
- is not ownership;
- does not unlock survey;
- does not auto-reveal hidden resources;
- does not trigger new-owner site-knowledge logic.

19. GENERATION ORDER / RNG DOMAIN CONTRACT
======================================================================

Required logical order:
1. fixed world/geography is already authoritative;
2. generate tracked opportunity counts;
3. select distinct opportunity types from explicit configured global weights;
4. apply configured soft anti-clustering;

RNG DOMAINS MUST BE INDEPENDENT

- campaign cast/naming generation;
- opportunity generation;
- opportunity hiding;
- starting countryside sites;
- starting strategic buildings;
- later survey commitment randomness when that runtime system is implemented.

Adding an RNG draw to one domain must not silently perturb another domain's generated
world.

REPRODUCIBILITY
Same campaign seed + same relevant generator/content version/configuration -> same
objective economy-start result.

Persist enough provenance to recreate/debug generation consistently without regenerating
saved authoritative state on load.

Generation-affecting changes require an appropriate generator/config version bump rather
than silently changing old-seed meaning.
